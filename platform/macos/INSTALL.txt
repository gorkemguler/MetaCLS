MetaCLS Drop — macOS 14 or later

1. Drag MetaCLS Drop.app to Applications.
2. Open it from Applications.
3. Drop PDF, Office, SVG or image files onto the window, or click Choose files.

Files are cleaned IN PLACE: originals are overwritten. Keep a copy if needed.
Nothing is uploaded. Python, pip, Homebrew and a separate ExifTool installation
are not required. ExifTool uses macOS's built-in /usr/bin/perl.

Community builds without an Apple Developer ID/notarization may be blocked on
first launch. After trying to open the app, go to System Settings > Privacy &
Security > Open Anyway, and confirm only if you trust this download.
Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

Apple Silicon (M-series): choose the arm64 download.
Intel Mac: choose the x86_64 download.

Some legacy Office files require conversion with LibreOffice, which is not
included. Unsupported files and cleaning errors appear in the results log.
To uninstall, quit and remove MetaCLS Drop.app from Applications.
